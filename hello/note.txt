To compile the hello.c:
riscv64-unknown-elf-g++ -o hello.elf hello.c     

To run it:
spike pk hello.elf

(conda activated and executed source env.sh)

The CycleTimer is directly reading from the Hardware-Cycle-Register, which means the whole number of cycles between two points in the program are teh result. To use the CycleTimer correctly, it is neccesary to know the CPU frequency.
